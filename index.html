About MIIISHOP

Small things, made slowly.

MIIISHOP began with a simple dream: I wanted to sew, even before I knew how. I started with no experience, but my friends gifted me my very first sewing machine, and I began practicing one stitch at a time. Little by little, I grew more confident, more creative, and more in love with the process.

After upgrading my machine, my hobby slowly turned into a small business. What started as a personal passion became something I was excited to share with others. I love making pieces that feel joyful, useful, and full of warmth, and I hope every item brings the same happiness to you that it brings to me when I create it.

Good to know

How big are they?

Mini wallet: approx. 11 × 8 cm. Mini pouch: approx. 13 × 9.5 cm. Both fit cards, coins, keys, lip balm and earbuds.

Does it come with a strap?

Yes — every order includes one paracord wristlet strap. Pick your colour at checkout from the product page.

How do I wash it?

Hand wash cold with mild soap, press between a towel and air dry flat. Don't tumble dry — the quilting will pucker.

Shipping & returns

Orders ship within 1–3 working days. Unused items can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
