About MIIISHOP

Small things, made slowly.

MIIISHOP started at a kitchen table with one sewing machine and a pile of leftover cotton. We make mini wallets and mini pouches — the kind of thing you reach for every single day without thinking about it.

Each piece is quilted in a diamond pattern for structure, lined inside, and finished with a metal zip and a woven MIIISHOP label. No two are identical, because fabric prints never line up the same way twice.

Good to know

How big are they?

Mini wallet: approx. 11 × 8 cm. Mini pouch: approx. 13 × 9.5 cm. Both fit cards, coins, keys, lip balm and earbuds.

Does it come with a strap?

Yes — every order includes one paracord wristlet strap. Pick your colour at checkout from the product page.

How do I wash it?

Hand wash cold with mild soap, press between a towel and air dry flat. Don't tumble dry — the quilting will pucker.

Shipping & returns

Orders ship within 1–3 working days. Unused items can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
