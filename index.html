About MIIISHOP

Small things, made slowly.

MIIISHOP began with a simple dream: I wanted to sew, even before I knew how. I started with no experience, but my friends gifted me my first sewing machine and I began learning one stitch at a time.

What started as a hobby slowly became a small handmade business. Every item is created with care, patience and a love for practical little pieces that feel personal, useful and full of character.

Good to know

How big are they?

Mini wallet: approx. 11 × 8 cm. Everyday pouch: approx. 13 × 9.5 cm. Both fit cards, coins, keys, lip balm and earbuds.

Does it come with a strap?

Yes — each piece includes a matching wristlet strap designed for easy everyday carry.

How do I wash it?

Hand wash cold with mild soap, press between a towel and air dry flat. Don't tumble dry — the quilting will pucker.

What makes each item different?

Prints vary slightly from one batch to the next, which adds to the handmade character and keeps each piece unique.
